How it works

Four steps from your routine to a shared ride

Every match comes from inside one of your circles. Classmates, neighbours, colleagues already heading the same direction.

01
Step 01

Set up your profile

Add your name, a home area, and whether you drive, ride, or both. Other users see block-level proximity, not your address.

02
Step 02

Join a circle

Circles are invitation-only groups: a school year, an apartment block, a workplace. You accept each one before it activates.

03
Step 03

Set your routine

Pick your commute days and a 30-minute departure window. 7:30–8:00 AM toward Raffles Place, for example. Different routines for different days work fine.

04
Step 04

Signal and go

When someone in your circle matches your window, you both get a notification. Tap Signal when you're heading out. Both signals in, you're connected.

Features

Built for how you commute

Every feature reduces the friction of commuting with someone you already know.

Trusted Circles

Your matching pool contains only people from your circles. A circle is a group you belong to: school cohort, apartment building, workplace. The app has no way to surface someone you don't share a circle with.

Commute Routines

Set recurring windows by day of week. When your window overlaps with someone from your circles, that's a match.

Live Window

When your window opens, the app goes live. Matches from your circles appear in real-time. You coordinate on the day, while you're getting ready.

Signal

One tap says you're heading out. When your match signals back, you both get notified. No threads, no scheduling.

One-Time Trips

Add a one-off trip for a specific date and window. Useful for events, appointments, or any day you want company but don't have a set routine.

Weekly Summary

Trips taken, trips shared, current streak. Your habits over time, built from the logs you record yourself.

Co-travel Streak

Track consecutive days you shared a commute with someone from your circles. A counter that shows whether shared commuting is becoming a habit.

Trip Log

After each window closes, log how it went: solo, shared, or skipped. Your patterns build over weeks. The app only knows what you record.

Silent Dismissals

Dismiss a match and it vanishes from your view. The other person gets no notification. You owe no explanation.

Why commute together

You commute every day.
Someone you know takes the same route.

CommuteCircle finds them.

01

Split transport costs

Two people in one taxi costs less than two taxis. The app logs distance, ERP gantries, and parking. Costs split proportionally and settle every Friday evening. Both sides see the itemised breakdown before money moves.

02

A familiar face makes the commute easier

Commuting alone at 7am or after dark is tiring. Travelling with someone you know, even just a neighbour, makes the commute easier to start. That familiarity builds over weeks.

03

Deepen connections you already have

The people in your circles are already part of your life. Commuting together is time you'd spend anyway, now spent with someone you know. The connection deepens without any scheduling.

04

Fewer vehicles on the road

Two people in one car is one fewer car on the road. The arithmetic is simple: a thousand people making this choice every morning removes hundreds of vehicles from peak-hour traffic.

Privacy by design

Specific decisions,
stated plainly.

The technical choices we made about your data, and why.

Block-level location only

You enter a home address as your departure default. Other users see block-level proximity only.

Phone hidden until both signal

Your mobile number reaches a matched commuter only once both of you signal for the same active trip. At any other time, no one on the platform can see it.

No public profile

Strangers cannot find you. Your profile exists only inside the circles you've joined. No one can search for you by name.

No background GPS tracking

CommuteCircle never requests GPS access. You enter your departure location yourself. The app has no knowledge of where you are at any moment.

Data minimisation

We collect what matching needs: commute windows, destination, circle memberships. We don't build advertising profiles. We don't sell data.

Silent dismissals

Dismiss a match and it vanishes from your view. The other person gets no notification. You owe no explanation.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · Canonical version ↗

1. What we collect

When you create a CommuteCircle account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address from your sign-in provider
  • Your mobile phone number — optional, entered during onboarding
  • Your home address — optional, used as departure default and stored at block level
  • Your commute routines: active days, departure windows, and destination
  • Your circle memberships
  • Trip log entries you voluntarily record after a commute window closes

2. How we use your data

  • To match you with people in your circles who share overlapping commute windows and destinations
  • To send in-app notifications when a match is found during your live window
  • To display your weekly commute summary and co-travel streak
  • To pre-calculate proximity within your circles so matches appear faster

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising or build advertising profiles
  • We do not track your real-time or background GPS location
  • We do not share your information with anyone outside CommuteCircle
  • We do not make your profile discoverable outside your circles

4. What a matched commuter can see

During an active match, another user in your circle can see:

  • Your name and approximate departure area — block level, not your exact address
  • Your destination
  • Whether you have signalled readiness to depart

Your phone number reaches a matched commuter only once both of you signal for the same active trip. Outside that window, no one on the platform can access it.

5. Third-party services

  • Authentication provider — handles secure sign-in; we do not store your password
  • Google Places API — used for address autocomplete during profile setup
  • Cloud infrastructure — your data is stored on access-controlled cloud servers

6. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Edit or remove your home address and phone number from your profile
  • Deactivate or delete individual commute routines
  • Leave any circle you are a member of
  • Request full deletion of your account and all associated personal data

7. Data retention

Active account data stays while your account exists. Trip logs and match history support your weekly summary and streak. We permanently remove personal data within 30 days of account deletion. We may retain anonymised records longer for accounting and regulatory purposes.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to production databases is restricted and logged. We follow standard practices for authentication, session management, and credential handling.

9. Changes to this policy

We update this policy as the product changes. Significant changes appear in-app before they take effect. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance.
