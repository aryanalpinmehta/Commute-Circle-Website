Introduction

Commute Circle ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Commute Circle.

This Privacy Policy applies to our app and its associated subdomains (collectively, our "Service") alongside our application, Commute Circle. By accessing or using our Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

We do not track users across third-party apps or websites, and we do not sell personal information. All data we collect is used solely to operate and improve the Commute Circle service.

Definitions and key terms

To help explain things as clearly as possible, every time any of these terms are referenced, they are strictly defined as:

  • Cookie: a small amount of data generated by a website and saved by your web browser. Used to support app functionality and remember information about you such as your language preference or login information.
  • Company: when this policy mentions "Company," "we," "us," or "our," it refers to Commute Circle, which is responsible for your information under this Privacy Policy.
  • Country: where Commute Circle or the owners/founders of Commute Circle are based — in this case, Singapore.
  • Device: any internet-connected device such as a phone, tablet, or computer that can be used to visit Commute Circle and use the Service.
  • IP address: a number assigned to every internet-connected device. IP addresses are usually assigned in geographic blocks and can often be used to identify the location from which a device is connecting to the Internet.
  • Personnel: individuals employed by Commute Circle or under contract to perform services on behalf of Commute Circle.
  • Personal Data: any information that directly, indirectly, or in connection with other information — including a personal identification number — allows for the identification or identifiability of a natural person.
  • Service: the service provided by Commute Circle as described in the relative terms (if available) and on this platform.
  • Third-party service: service providers who help us operate the app, such as hosting, database, or maps and location services.
  • App / Application: the Commute Circle app — the software product identified above.
  • You: a person or entity registered with Commute Circle to use the Service.

What information do we collect?

We collect information from you when you register on our app or use the Service:

  • Name — used to display your profile to circle members and for commute matching.
  • Email address — used to create and secure your account and for service notifications.
  • Phone number (optional) — used for in-app contact between commuters who have been matched.
  • Home area / address (approximate) — the general area you enter is used to match you with nearby commuters heading the same way. We do not collect or store your precise GPS location, and it is never continuously tracked.

This information is used exclusively for app functionality — specifically, account creation, commute matching, and communication between circle members. It is not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

When does Commute Circle use end-user information from third parties?

Commute Circle collects the end-user data necessary to provide the Service directly from you. Your account is authenticated through Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email and password — we do not use any government identity-verification service, and we do not collect social media profile data unless you explicitly choose to connect a social account.

When does Commute Circle use customer information from third parties?

We may receive limited information from third-party fraud-detection or abuse-prevention services when you register, to help us maintain a safe, verified community. We do not collect or purchase data from data brokers or marketing partners.

Do we share the information we collect with third parties?

We may share the information we collect only with trusted service providers who help us operate the app — such as hosting, database, and maps and location services. All such providers are contractually required to handle your data securely and only for the purpose of providing services to us. We do not sell personal information.

We may also disclose personal and non-personal information about you to government or law enforcement officials or private parties as we, in our sole discretion, believe necessary or appropriate to respond to claims and legal process (including subpoenas), to protect our rights and interests or those of a third party, the safety of the public or any person, to prevent or stop any illegal, unethical, or legally actionable activity, or to otherwise comply with applicable court orders, laws, rules, and regulations.

Where and when is information collected from customers and end users?

Commute Circle collects personal information that you submit to us during registration and use of the app. We may also receive limited personal information about you from third-party fraud-prevention services as described above.

How do we use the information we collect?

Any of the information we collect from you may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account, and display your name to circle members.
  • Authenticate you via Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password.
  • Match you with nearby commuters whose routes overlap yours.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • Send service notifications and match or commute updates.
  • Improve the reliability and safety of the app based on anonymised usage patterns.

How do we use your email address?

By submitting your email address on this app, you agree to receive service-related emails from us. These include account confirmations, match notifications, and support replies. You can unsubscribe from optional communications at any time by clicking the opt-out link in any email. We only send emails to people who have authorised us to contact them. We do not send unsolicited commercial emails.

Your email address is used only for service communications directly related to your Commute Circle account and matches. We do not use email addresses for advertising audience targeting or share them with marketing partners.

Note: If at any time you would like to unsubscribe from non-essential emails, we include detailed unsubscribe instructions at the bottom of each email.

How long do we keep your information?

We keep your information only so long as we need it to provide Commute Circle to you and fulfil the purposes described in this policy. This is also the case for anyone we share your information with and who carries out services on our behalf. When we no longer need to use your information and there is no need for us to keep it to comply with our legal or regulatory obligations, we'll either remove it from our systems or depersonalise it so that we can't identify you.

When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 60 days.

How do we protect your information?

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. All sensitive information is transmitted using industry-standard encryption in transit. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only, and we regularly review our security practices.

We cannot, however, guarantee the absolute security of any information you transmit to Commute Circle. We will notify you in a timely manner if a data breach occurs that may affect your rights or freedoms.

Could my information be transferred to other countries?

Commute Circle is incorporated in Singapore. Information collected via our app may be processed by our hosting or service providers, who may be located in other countries. We ensure that any international transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, including contractual protections, consistent with applicable data protection laws.

Can I update or correct my information?

You have the right to request updates, corrections, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. You can exercise these rights by:

  • Updating your profile directly within the app.
  • Contacting us at hello@commutecircle.app with your request.
  • Deleting your account via Settings → Account → Delete account.

We will respond to all requests within one month. To protect your privacy and security, we may verify your identity before making corrections.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to support site functionality and remember your preferences. A cookie is a small piece of data stored on your computer or mobile device by your web browser. We use cookies to keep your session working correctly and to remember settings such as language preferences. We never place personally identifiable information in cookies.

You may set your browser to block cookies, but doing so may prevent parts of our website from working correctly. Different browsers make different controls available — see your browser's help menu for more information.

We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies of any kind.

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), this section explains how we handle your data under GDPR.

What is GDPR? GDPR is an EU-wide privacy and data protection law that gives EU residents enhanced control over their personal data. We apply GDPR standards as a baseline for all our operations worldwide, regardless of where our users are located.

Legal basis for processing. We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing your name, email, and phone number is necessary to provide the Commute Circle service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — improving app reliability and preventing fraud.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records required by Singapore law or applicable regulation.

Individual data subject rights. Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data. We store personal data for up to 6 years unless your account is deleted, after which data is removed within 60 days. To exercise your rights, contact us at hello@commutecircle.app.

California residents

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants California residents additional rights over their personal information, including:

  • Right to know and access. You may request information about the categories of personal information we collect, how we use it, and the specific pieces we hold about you.
  • Right to equal service. We will not discriminate against you if you exercise your privacy rights.
  • Right to delete. You may request deletion of your personal information by deleting your account or contacting us.
  • Right to opt out of sale. We do not sell your personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond.
